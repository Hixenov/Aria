# 0.2.6 manual check: the short one

Since 0.2.0 every release has left a list of checks for a person, and most of their lines are still
pending: about 140 in all. This list picks out about 40 of them, the ones that would tell the most,
plus the new behaviour of 0.2.6. It should take about 20 minutes, or about 30 with a voice chat.
The full lists stay where they are, for detail:
[phase2a](phase2a-manual-check.md), [phase2b](phase2b-manual-check.md),
[phase2c](phase2c-manual-check.md), [music to chat](music-to-chat-manual-check.md) and
[0.2.4](0.2.4-manual-check.md).

Each line says who verified it:

- **pending**: not verified by anyone yet. Replace it with `user` and the date when you have
  checked it, or write what went wrong.
- **agent**: run by the coding agent, with no sound judged.

## Before you start

1. Close Aria.
2. Copy your data folder somewhere safe: `%APPDATA%\Aria` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Aria`.
3. Have at hand: a few MP3 and FLAC files, an album whose tracks flow into each other (a live or
   classical one), one track at 44.1 kHz and one at 48 kHz, headphones you can unplug, and for the
   chat part, VB-CABLE and a chat program (Discord, for example) with someone to listen.
