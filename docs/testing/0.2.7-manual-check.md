# 0.2.7 manual check

What 0.2.7 adds can only be tried for real in a running Windows: Explorer's double-click and "Open
with", a drop from Explorer, and the system's change notifications on your own folders. It should
take about 15 minutes. The short check of everything before it is in
[0.2.6-manual-check.md](0.2.6-manual-check.md).

Each line says who verified it:

- **pending**: not verified by anyone yet. Replace it with `user` and the date when you have
  checked it, or write what went wrong.
- **agent**: run by the coding agent.

## Before you start

1. Close Aria, and copy `%APPDATA%\Aria` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Aria` somewhere safe.
2. Install `Aria-0.2.7-setup.exe` over the installed version.
3. Have at hand an MP3 or FLAC that is in your library, one that is not (in Downloads, say), a
   text file, and a folder of music that is not in your library.
